a note from the author

Why this site exists

Most students do not lose marks because maths is hard. They lose marks because a step was skipped in class, a figure was never drawn, or nobody showed them what a full-marks answer looks like. This site fixes those three things, chapter by chapter, and asks for nothing in return.

Keep your NCERT book open next to it. Question numbers here match the book exactly, so you can move between the two without losing your place. Tick questions off as you finish them, star the ones that beat you, and come back to the stars before the exam.

— Dr. PK

  • Works on a phone, and in dark “blackboard” mode (the moon button on top).
  • Your ticks and stars are stored only in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
